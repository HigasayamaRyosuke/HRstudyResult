abstract: "In recent years, motion representation that incorporates physical levels of exertion has gained attention as a technique to enhance the visual impact and expressiveness of character animation and video production. However, conventional motion capture primarily focuses on outward kinematic movements, and motion blending implemented in standard CG systems typically relies on simple linear interpolation of joint angles. Therefore, in this study, we achieve natural motion synthesis by representing joint angles as Rodrigues vectors and applying Laplacian editing. This enables the stitching of multiple different motions into a single sequence and the fusion of different types of actions."
